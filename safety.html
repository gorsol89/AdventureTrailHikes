🚨 Health & Safety

🔍

Be Prepared

Adventure Trails prioritizes your enjoyment, which relies on your safety and well-being. While we do our best to ensure a pleasurable trek, it's important to prepare yourself. Read up on your upcoming trip, check the links we provide, and ensure you have suitable clothing and footwear.

⛰️

Be Realistic

Scaling a 2,000-meter summit might seem doable from your living room, but make sure you're physically ready for the challenge! It's tempting to book the hardest trail for your first hike, but starting with something easier might be wiser. If you have a medical condition, consider that, and ensure vaccinations, if necessary.

🌍

Be Humble

Respect cultural differences while traveling. Certain forms of dress or behavior may not align with local customs. Show respect for the environment by following our "leave a place tidier than you found it" principle, ensuring the natural world can be enjoyed by future travelers too.
