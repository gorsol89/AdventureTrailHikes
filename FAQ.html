❓ FAQ

📅

How do I book a tour?

You can book a tour by visiting our Booking page or by calling our office directly at 55 55 58 56. We’re here to help you choose the best adventure suited for your preferences.

💲

Prices

Our tour prices are customized based on group size, itinerary, and specific needs. Because of this, prices may vary. Please contact us for detailed information

🎒

What do I need to bring?

We recommend that hikers come prepared with appropriate clothing and gear for the trip. Essential items include durable hiking shoes, weather-appropriate clothing, and personal supplies. Don’t worry if you forget something – we can provide extra equipment at an additional cost.

🛡️

Insurance

To ensure peace of mind during your adventure, we offer a comprehensive insurance package that covers all aspects of the tour, including medical emergencies and equipment loss. Reach out to us for more details on the coverage options.

♿

Accessibility

We are committed to providing memorable experiences for all travelers, including those with disabilities. Our tours offer accessible routes and accommodations. Contact us for specific information regarding accessibility options and to tailor your experience.
